<h1>Privacy Policy for “Whimzy” by Good Folk Digital</h1>

Effective Date: Sep 27, 2025

Good Folk Digital ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application, Whimzy (the "App"). Please read this policy carefully to understand our views and practices regarding your personal data and how we will treat it.

<h2>1. Information We Collect</h2> <h3>a. Personal Information</h3> Whimzy does not require you to create an account to play, but we may collect limited personal information if you choose to interact with certain features:

Login Credentials (Optional): If you create an account, we use Firebase Authentication to securely manage your login credentials. Your password is encrypted and obfuscated.

Email Address (Optional): If you sign up, we may use your email to provide support, send updates about your account, or respond to inquiries.

<h3>b. Usage Data</h3> We collect information about your interactions with the App, including:

Device Information: Device type, operating system, and other unique identifiers.

Usage Patterns: Firebase Analytics tracks which features you use, how you interact with decks, and gameplay activity to help us improve the experience.

Crash Data: Firebase Crashlytics collects crash reports and diagnostic data so we can identify and fix issues.

<h3>c. In-App Purchases</h3> When you make a purchase (such as unlocking a Deck or Premium), Google Play handles all payment processing. We do not have access to or store your payment details. <h2>2. How We Use Your Information</h2> We use the information we collect in the following ways:

To Improve Gameplay: Analytics helps us understand how players use the App so we can refine the experience.

To Communicate With You: If you provide contact details, we may send you support responses or updates about your purchases.

To Maintain Stability: Crash and error reports help us keep Whimzy stable and reliable.

<h2>3. Data Sharing and Disclosure</h2> We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share limited information in the following cases:

Service Providers: Trusted third parties like Firebase provide authentication, analytics, and crash reporting.

Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required to comply with applicable laws or lawful government requests.

<h2>4. In-App Purchases and Ads</h2>

One-Time Purchases: All purchases in Whimzy are one-time unlocks. We do not use subscriptions or recurring billing.

Free Rewards: You may earn free custom cards and rewards by playing mini-games or visiting the App.

Advertisements: Free versions of Whimzy may display ads. Ads are served by trusted third-party networks that may collect anonymized data to personalize content.

<h2>5. Data Security</h2>

We prioritize the security of your data. We use encryption and Firebase’s built-in security measures to help protect your information against unauthorized access.

<h2>6. Your Rights</h2>

You may request to access, update, or delete your personal information at any time by contacting us at support@goodfolkdigital.com
